The Certificate

AI is already at work. Someone has to manage it.

Intelligent systems already shape decisions in finance, healthcare, logistics, and marketing, and their role keeps growing. Leading that work takes more than knowing the tools. It takes judgment about where AI creates value, how to build it, and how to govern it.

The Stevens graduate certificate in Management of Artificial Intelligence teaches you to analyze business opportunities, work with data and models, explain machine-generated results to decision makers, and bring AI into everyday processes. Governance, data regulation, security, and privacy are treated as core skills, not afterthoughts.

Every student takes the same two core courses, then two more in the track that fits their role.

Follow the Track
Management Track

From pain point to pitch.

The management track runs in sequence. The shared core comes first. Then two project courses in which a team carries one AI product from the first user interview to an executive pitch.

1
BIA 568 + BIA 662

Foundations

Learn how AI systems work, where they fail, and how to govern them. Build your first generative AI applications.

Governance judgment and hands-on generative AI skills
2
MIS 676

Discover & Prototype

Start from pain points in real work. Interview users, find the insight, brainstorm, and build a working AI prototype with AI coding tools.

A working prototype and a pitch video
3
MIS 678

Realize

Test the prototype with users and add explainability, trust, and error handling. Then build what a launch needs: a business model, governance, architecture, and a change plan, and pitch it to an executive panel.

A business case pitched to leadership
Courses

Six courses. You take four.

Two shared core courses, plus the two courses of your track. Every course is 3 credits.

BIA 568
Shared core

Management of AI Technologies

AI applications across business and technology, from fraud detection and machine translation to facial recognition and autonomous vehicles. In each area the focus is on managing AI projects: fairness, accountability, transparency, ethics, and the law.

RoleThe governance anchor of the certificate.
BIA 662
Shared core

Augmented Intelligence & Generative AI

How augmented intelligence and generative AI change evidence-based decision making: design principles, natural language processing, knowledge representation, and deep learning architectures. Students build business applications.

RoleThe hands-on generative AI course, used in both tracks.
MIS 637
Technical track

Data Analytics & Machine Learning

Data mining and machine learning applied to real business and operations problems: data preparation, nearest-neighbor methods, decision trees, neural networks, clustering, and model evaluation.

RoleTurns company data into models managers can act on.
BIA 667
Technical track

Deep Learning & Business Applications

Neural networks from the ground up, then convolutional and recurrent networks, generative models, and reinforcement learning, with business cases and the risks of putting deep learning to work.

SequenceBest taken after MIS 637.
MIS 676
Management track

Human-Centered Design of AI Artifacts

A full human-centered design process adapted to AI products and services. Teams interview users, synthesize insights, brainstorm, and build a working prototype with AI coding tools. Students without coding experience build one too.

PrerequisitesBIA 568 and BIA 662
MIS 678
Management track

Realization of AI Artifacts

Teams take their prototype into an organizational setting: more user research, a business model canvas, a governance and architecture framework, and a change management plan. Ends with a pitch to an executive panel.

PrerequisitesMIS 676

For Organizations

Bring a cohort. Solve your own problems.

The management track can run as a dedicated cohort for a single organization. Your people learn by designing AI products for problems they already face at work, and they present the results to your leaders.
