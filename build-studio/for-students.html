Four Years · Build Studio

Undergraduate Pathway

From designing agents, to designing for people, to building with partners, to the capstone.

Year 1
MIS 201 · Redesigned

Fundamentals of Agentic Information Systems

How organizations use information systems, rebuilt around agents: agents, tools, workflows and data. Students model business processes and design agents that act within them.

Design the agent
Concepts · agents as system components
Year 2
New course

Agentic Design

Designing agentic systems with people built in: oversight and hand-off points, accountability, trust, and how work is reorganized around agents.

Design for people
Sociotechnical design · humans in the loop
Year 3 · Two-semester sequence
Fall · 3 cr

Prototype Studio I

AI Product Discovery & Prototyping

Partners bring real problems. Teams run requirements and design sprints, then build and evaluate working AI prototypes. Partner check-in at semester end.

Spring · 3 cr

Prototype Studio II

AI Product Deployment & Evaluation

The same teams take their prototypes into a pilot with the partner: hardening, red-teaming, governance and handoff, ending with a final presentation to partners.

Practice · real partners, real users
Year 4
Capstone

Senior Design

Year-long capstone. Students arrive having completed a full design-build-deploy cycle with a real partner, ready to lead larger, more independent agentic system projects.

Lead the build
Integration · independent capstone

Prototype Studio I is open to undergraduates in all majors and is the prerequisite for Prototype Studio II.

Graduate Pathway

Design, realize, then build it for real

Two design courses take an AI product from first user interview to a full business case. Then choose one practicum to apply it.

Step 1
MIS 676

Human-Centered Design of AI Artifacts

Run a human-centered design process end to end: research users, ideate, and build a first working AI prototype, then present it for feedback.

Design
Users first · prototype to feedback
Step 2
MIS 678

Realization of AI Artifacts

Take your MIS 676 prototype further: more user research; explainability, trust and error handling; a business model canvas, governance and architecture framework, and change-management plan. Ends with a pitch to executives and experts.

Realize
Business case · from app to adoption
Step 3 · Choose one practicum
BIA 679

Big Data Practicum

Build an end-to-end data pipeline for a business problem and deliver it as a data product.

or
BIA 686

Practicum in Analytics

Model a real business problem with text, descriptive, predictive and prescriptive analytics.

or
MIS 702

Practicum in Information Systems

A supervised company project with a faculty advisor and an industry mentor.

Apply · a real project, your choice

MIS 678 requires MIS 676 and at least 12 credits in the MSIS or BI&A program (or BIA 568 and BIA 662 in the Management of AI graduate certificate). Practicum prerequisites vary — check the Stevens course catalog and your advisor.
