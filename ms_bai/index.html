Program Overview
Program Educational Objectives

What graduates will achieve 3–5 years after graduation:

  1. Lead AI initiatives, from use case identification to deployed system, in enterprises, consulting firms, and technology organizations.
  2. Combine analytical, technical, and organizational judgment to decide where AI creates value and how to build it.
  3. Advance responsible AI practice, contributing to governance, accountability, and ethical use of AI in organizations.
  4. Progress into senior roles, such as AI product lead, AI strategy director, or analytics and AI manager.
Program Educational Outcomes

What students will be able to do upon graduation:

  1. Manage and prepare enterprise data for analytics and AI applications.
  2. Build machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI solutions for business problems.
  3. Lead AI adoption in the business, with depth in AI enablement, AI data engineering, or data science.
  4. Govern AI projects responsibly, addressing fairness, accountability, transparency, ethics, and law.
  5. Communicate AI designs, decisions, and impact to technical and non-technical audiences.
  6. Deliver a working AI system for a real sponsor organization through the three-phase Build Studio.
Intended Audience

The program sits between a management-of-IS degree and a quantitative analytics degree. It is for people who want to build with AI and lead its use in the business.

  • Recent graduates in business, IS, CS, engineering, or analytics.
  • Early-career professionals in IT, consulting, product, or operations moving into AI roles.
  • Working professionals building the degree one certificate at a time, including corporate cohorts.

Basic Python required; calculus not required. An optional pre-program bootcamp covers Python, SQL, and statistics.

The degree — three stackable graduate certificates · click any certificate or course

Each certificate stands on its own and is listed on the transcript. Completing the Management of AI certificate, the Forward Deployed AI Engineering certificate, and any one depth certificate within the degree time limit earns the MS in Business + AI. Part-time students can stop after any certificate and come back later.

1 · Management of AIFoundation · 12 credits · all four courses · existing certificate

The AI foundation: machine learning, generative AI, deep learning, and how to manage and govern AI projects. This is the existing Stevens certificate, including the version delivered to corporate cohorts. It carries into the degree in full.

2 · Forward Deployed AI EngineeringPractice · 9 credits · Build Studio, three phases in order

Build Studio as a certificate. Students embed with a sponsor organization, frame an AI opportunity, build and govern a working system, and deploy it in the field. That is the work of a forward deployed engineer. Working professionals can use their own employer as the sponsor. Prerequisite: the Management of AI certificate or equivalent experience building with AI.

3 · Depth certificate — choose one9 credits · MIS 630 + two courses

Every depth certificate starts with MIS 630 Managing the Data Resource, the data foundation shared by MSIS and BA&AI. The other two courses set the direction: AI Enablement makes the degree closer to the MS in Information Systems, and Data Science for AI and AI Data Engineering make it closer to the MS in Business Analytics & AI.
