Program Overview
Program Educational Objectives

What graduates will achieve 3–5 years after graduation:

  1. Lead AI initiatives, from use case identification to deployed system, in enterprises, consulting firms, and technology organizations.
  2. Combine analytical, technical, and organizational judgment to decide where AI creates value and how to build it.
  3. Advance responsible AI practice, contributing to governance, accountability, and ethical use of AI in organizations.
  4. Progress into senior roles, such as AI product lead, AI strategy director, or analytics and AI manager.
Program Educational Outcomes

What students will be able to do upon graduation:

  1. Manage and prepare enterprise data for analytics and AI applications.
  2. Build machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI solutions for business problems.
  3. Lead AI adoption in the business, with added depth in either IS strategy and process or advanced analytics.
  4. Govern AI projects responsibly, addressing fairness, accountability, transparency, ethics, and law.
  5. Communicate AI designs, decisions, and impact to technical and non-technical audiences.
  6. Deliver a working AI system for a real sponsor organization through the three-phase Build Studio.
Intended Audience

The program sits between a management-of-IS degree and a quantitative analytics degree. It is for people who want to build with AI and lead its use in the business.

  • Recent graduates in business, IS, CS, engineering, or analytics.
  • Early-career professionals in IT, consulting, product, or operations moving into AI roles.
  • Working professionals, including corporate cohorts, continuing from the Management of AI certificate.

Basic Python required; calculus not required. An optional pre-program bootcamp covers Python, SQL, and statistics.

Electives — choose two · lean toward MSIS or toward BA&AI

Students choose two electives from a short list. Both from the IS list makes the degree closer to the MS in Information Systems. Both from the analytics list makes it closer to the MS in Business Analytics & AI. One from each gives a balanced profile.

Management of AI certificate — built in

Earn the certificate on the way to the degree

The four Management of AI certificate courses are required courses in Business + AI. Every student completes the certificate before graduating. Part-time students complete it in their first two terms.

Professionals who complete the certificate first, including corporate cohorts, carry all 12 credits into the degree and need 18 more, including the full Build Studio. Their Build Studio project can be sponsored by their own employer.
